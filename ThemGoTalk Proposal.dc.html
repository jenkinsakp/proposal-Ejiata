ThemGoTalk

A Proposal for Transparent Election Information and Evidence Management

ThemGoTalk is designed as an independent, non-partisan election-transparency platform that helps citizens, political parties, and observer organisations collect, review, and organise polling-unit result evidence.

See the platform Express interest

01 — The Need

During elections, result sheets and field submissions may be scattered across paper files, messaging platforms, and separate teams.

This can make it difficult to monitor reporting coverage, review disputed records, and maintain a clear, organised evidence trail.

04 — Organisational Situation Room

An organisation's results workspace serves as its in-house Situation Room. It gives authorised teams one place to review field submissions, monitor reporting coverage, organise result evidence, and prepare internal reports.

ThemGoTalk can be provided as a managed service, with an optional dedicated private deployment for an organisation that requires its own server environment. This option would be subject to agreed hosting, security, support, and data-governance arrangements. It would not give any organisation control over ThemGoTalk's public results or independent review process.

06 — Neutrality and Scope

ThemGoTalk is designed to serve participating political parties and observer groups on equal terms. Its purpose is to support election transparency and evidence review while remaining neutral and non-partisan.

07 — Pre-Launch Status

ThemGoTalk is currently in a private pre-launch phase and has not yet been introduced to the general public. We are sharing this early overview with a small number of relevant stakeholders to gather informed feedback before its public launch. This early engagement does not provide exclusivity or preferential access and does not imply political endorsement.

Important Disclaimer

ThemGoTalk is an independent platform and is not an official INEC platform. Election result information displayed by ThemGoTalk may be independently processed and aggregated from publicly available information published through INEC's IReV portal and from result sheets submitted by participating political party agents. Agent-submitted information is identified separately by its source and review status. All results displayed are unofficial and do not constitute INEC's official collation or declaration of election results.
